# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

## Overview

**MiFormulas** is a personal note app for writing and keeping math formulas together with text. This policy describes how the app stores, uses, and protects data.

MiFormulas has no app account, no developer server, no analytics, and no ads. All notes are stored on your device, and the developer cannot see them.

---

## 1. Data Collection

### 1.1 Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores only the following:

| Data Type | How It Is Collected | Purpose | Where It Is Stored |
|---------|--------|------|-------|
| Notes (title and body) | Entered by the user | Saving and searching notes | The app's storage on the device |
| Created and updated time of each note | Recorded automatically | Sorting the list, comparing notes when restoring a backup | The app's storage on the device |
| Whether Premium has been purchased | Recorded automatically on purchase | Keeping Premium available when the store cannot be reached | The app's storage on the device |
| Time of the last backup | Recorded automatically on backup | Showing it in Settings to encourage backups | The app's storage on the device |

### 1.2 Data We Do Not Collect

We **never collect** the following:

- ❌ Personally identifiable information such as name, address, phone number, or email address
- ❌ Account or authentication information (the app has no accounts)
- ❌ Location
- ❌ Device IDs (including for advertising or analytics)
- ❌ Usage data (launches, time spent, features used, etc.)
- ❌ Crash reports
- ❌ Cookies or tracking

---

## 2. Data Storage

### 2.1 On-Device Storage

**All notes are stored in the app's storage on your device.**

- **Location**: Inside the iOS / Android app sandbox
- **Owner**: You (deleting the app also deletes the notes on the device)

### 2.2 Cloud Sync

The current version has no cloud sync. The app never sends your notes off the device.

### 2.3 OS Backups

iOS and Android may back up app data to your own account. This is a feature of the OS, and the app does not receive the data.

| Platform | Mechanism | What Happens |
|------------|--------|------|
| iOS | iCloud Backup (when turned on in the device settings) | Your MiFormulas notes are included in the backup of the whole device |
| Android | Android Auto Backup (when turned on in the device settings) | Your MiFormulas notes are backed up to your Google account, and come back when you reinstall the app with the same account |

In both cases the data is stored in your own Apple / Google account, and the developer cannot access it. The privacy policies of those companies apply (see 3.2).

### 2.4 Security of Data in Transit

| Item | Method |
|-----|------|
| On-device storage | OS sandbox, device encryption, and screen lock (iOS / Android) |
| OS backups | Encrypted connections provided by Apple / Google |
| Purchases | Encrypted connections provided by the App Store / Google Play |

---

## 3. Data Sharing

### 3.1 Sharing with Third Parties

**We do not share data with third parties.** The developer cannot access your notes.

The app connects to the network only when it talks to the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) to purchase Premium or restore a purchase.

### 3.2 Platform Privacy Policies

The following privacy policies also apply to OS backups and purchases:

- **Apple (iOS)**: [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Google (Android)**: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 3.3 Purchase Information

MiFormulas Premium (a one-time purchase) is bought through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android), and payment information (such as credit card numbers) is handled by Apple / Google. The developer does not collect or store personal information such as the buyer's name or email address in the app.

- **iOS**: The developer cannot obtain information that identifies individual buyers through App Store Connect (only aggregated sales data is available)
- **Android**: Because of how Google Play works, the developer may be able to see a buyer's email address and similar details on the Order management page of the Google Play Console, for handling refunds and orders

### 3.4 Feedback Email

Send feedback in Settings only opens your mail app with a draft addressed to the developer. The app never sends email itself.

- To help answer your question, the end of the draft includes lines with the app version and OS version. You can delete them before sending
- You decide whether to send it. The developer uses emails you send only to respond to you and to improve the app, and does not share them with third parties

### 3.5 Backup Files

Create backup in Settings saves all notes as a JSON file and hands it, through the share sheet, to a place you choose (Files, cloud storage, email, etc.). You choose where it goes, and neither the app nor the developer receives the file.

## 4. Your Rights

### 4.1 Right of Access

You can access your data in the following ways:

1. **In the app**: View your notes in the list and the editor
2. **Backup file**: Export all notes in JSON format

### 4.2 Right to Deletion

You can delete your data in the following ways:

| Method | Scope | Timing |
|-----|------|---------|
| Deleting a note in the app | The selected note (deleted after you confirm, and cannot be undone) | Immediately |
| Restoring a backup with Replace | Replaces the notes on the device with the backup, deleting notes that are not in the backup | When restoring |
| Uninstalling the app | All data on the device | When the app is deleted |

Data in OS backups (see 2.3) can be deleted from each OS's settings.

### 4.3 Right to Data Portability

You can export your data at any time:

```
[Settings] → [Data] → [Create backup]
```

The exported file is in JSON format and can be processed by other tools.

When restoring a backup, you can choose either:

- **Merge**: Keeps your current notes and adds the backup. When the same note is in both, the one updated more recently is kept
- **Replace**: Replaces your current notes with the backup

## 5. Security Measures

### 5.1 Current Measures

- ✅ **On-device storage**: Protected by the OS sandbox
- ✅ **Network communication**: Only with the App Store / Google Play for purchases
- ✅ **Access control**: Only the device owner can access the data
- ✅ **Offline support**: No dependence on a server

### 5.2 Encryption

The app does not add its own encryption. Data is protected by the following mechanisms provided by the OS and platforms:

- **Data on the device**: OS sandbox, device encryption, and screen lock (iOS / Android)
- **OS backups and purchases**: Encryption provided by Apple / Google

### 5.3 Reporting Security Issues

If you find a security issue, please report it privately to the following email address rather than in a public issue:

```
support (at) netplan.co.jp
```
