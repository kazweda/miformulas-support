# MiFormulas

**Write math beautifully and easily on your phone, and keep it**

---

## What You Can Do

- **Text and formulas in one note**: Just wrap math in `$...$`, and check how it looks in the preview
- **Math keypad**: Type numbers, symbols and Greek letters without switching keyboards
- **Formula guide**: Pick how to write fractions, integrals, matrices, cases and more
- **Search**: Find notes quickly by title and body

---

## Your Data Stays with You

- Notes are stored on your device
- No sign-up, no tracking, no ads
- Works without a network
- Back up all notes to a JSON file from Settings

Deleting the app also deletes the notes on your device. Back up the notes that matter to you from time to time.

---

## Pricing

Free to start.

- **Free**: Keep up to 30 notes
- **Premium (one-time purchase)**: Buy once, and keep as many notes as you like

There is no subscription. Purchases are made through the App Store / Google Play.
